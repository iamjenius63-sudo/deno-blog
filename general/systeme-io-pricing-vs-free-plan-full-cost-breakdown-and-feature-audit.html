The Hidden Costs of Legacy Software Add-Ons

When comparing software costs, most entrepreneurs only examine base subscription pricing. However, legacy platforms frequently require expensive additional plugins and third-party tools to achieve basic operational functionality:

  • Third-Party Video Hosting (Vimeo Pro): Kajabi and WordPress often require external video hosting subscriptions costing $240 to $600 per year. In Systeme.io, video streaming and hosting are 100% included for free.
  • API Integration Bridges (Zapier): Connecting ClickFunnels to external autoresponders requires Zapier plans starting at $360 to $900 per year. In Systeme.io, all components are natively linked with zero API webhooks.
  • SSL & Web Hosting: Maintaining a self-hosted website on WordPress costs $120 to $360 per year in hosting and security renewals. Systeme.io includes enterprise AWS cloud hosting and automated SSL certificates at zero cost.

When Should You Upgrade from Free to Paid?

To operate at maximum efficiency, only upgrade your account when your business reaches specific revenue or operational milestones:

  • Automated Evergreen Webinars: If your sales strategy relies on automated webinar presentations to sell high-ticket offers ($997+), upgrading to the Webinar plan ($47/mo) is mandatory.
  • Full Agency Migration: If you are migrating a large existing business from ClickFunnels or Kajabi, upgrading to the annual Unlimited plan ($828/yr) provides free done-for-you technical migration, saving weeks of manual work.
